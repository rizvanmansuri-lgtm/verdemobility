Why Mini DC

Built for India's
charging future.

Every detail engineered for real-world commercial deployment — from monsoon-proof casing to seamless CMS integration.

Dual-Output Power

Simultaneously charge two EVs up to 120 kW total. Intelligent power splitting ensures each vehicle charges at optimum speed without compromise.

7/10" TFT Touchscreen

Large TFT LCD touch screen with RGB LED visual indicators. Authenticate via mobile app, RFID card, QR code, or password. Configure via app or web portal for full operator control.

Multi-Layer Safety

8 built-in protection layers: over-voltage, over-current, under-voltage, surge, over-temperature, ground fault, polarity reverse, insulation monitoring, leakage detection, and cable temperature detection. Mushroom-type emergency stop as standard.

Quad Connectivity

GSM/3G/4G, Wi-Fi, Ethernet, and Bluetooth — all four built in. OCPP 1.6J standard with a field-upgradable path to OCPP 2.1 via OTA update. No dropped sessions, no lost revenue.

Dual CCS2 Output

Two CCS2 (IEC 62196.3-2014) connectors with 15 ft. standard cables. Supports IEC 61851, ISO 15118, and DIN 70121 vehicle protocols — compatible with all major EVs in India.

Advanced Load Management

Built-in energy meter with support for external energy meter integration and dynamic load management. Ready for integration with advanced smart building energy management systems.
