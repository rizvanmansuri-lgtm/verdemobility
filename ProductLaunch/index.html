Both models require a 3-phase 415V AC (±15%), 50 Hz supply — wired as L1, L2, L3, N, PE. The 120 kW Pro draws a maximum of 200A (120A per gun when both are in simultaneous operation). Our team will confirm your site's sanctioned load during the free site assessment.
Yes — the 120 kW Pro model supports simultaneous dual-vehicle charging. Power is intelligently split between both connectors, ensuring both EVs charge optimally based on demand.
The Mini DC features 2 × CCS2 (IEC 62196.3-2014) connectors, supporting IEC 61851, ISO 15118, and DIN 70121 vehicle protocols — compatible with Tata Nexon EV, Tata Punch EV, MG ZS EV, Kia EV6, BYD Atto 3, and all major CCS2-capable EVs in India.
The charger has quad-connectivity failover: GSM/3G/4G, Wi-Fi, Ethernet, and Bluetooth. If all network connections are lost, it operates in offline mode — local RFID/card authentication continues and sessions are queued and synced automatically when connectivity resumes.
Yes. The Mini DC Fast Charger is ARAI type-approved. All required certifications for DISCOM integration and government subsidy eligibility are provided with every unit.
The warranty covers all hardware components and factory defects with on-site repair or replacement within 48 hours. The 120 kW Pro includes remote diagnostics to identify issues proactively before they cause downtime.
